Our Story

A little corner where Trenton slows down.

Corner Coffee is a small, family-run café on Court Street, just minutes from Cloudland Canyon. We pull every shot by hand, cook breakfast to order, and know our regulars by name — and their order before they say it.

  • Locally roasted
    We proudly serve Goodman Coffee, roasted right down the road in Chattanooga, TN.
  • Made fresh, daily
    Breakfast wraps, paninis, waffles and pastries prepared every morning.
  • A corner to linger
    Cozy tables, free wi-fi, patio seating and a crowd that feels like family.
